\section*{Ethics and data protection}

The University of Buea Faculty of Engineering and Technology departmental research ethics review issued a non-interventional technical-study determination for the collection of operational, performance and access-control traces (FET-CE-2025-014, 14 July 2025). Participating farm operators and administrators provided written informed consent covering data collection, scientific analysis and public release of pseudonymized traces. Participation was voluntary. The released data contain no names, contact information or free-text fields identifying an individual; the outage log's technical notes describe equipment and network events only. User and sensor identifiers were replaced at collection, and the correspondence between identifiers and individuals was destroyed before preparation of the public dataset. Precise field coordinates were not collected in the released package; the site is reported only at settlement level in the manuscript text. We independently scanned the analyst-supplied raw-data package for names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise coordinates and other free-text fields as part of preparing this version (\texttt{analysis/DATA\_QUALITY\_REPORT.md}) and found none.
